# DeckProbe

DeckProbe is the discovery and inspection layer of the Deckflow product family. It answers targeted questions about a document without first building a complete page-and-element model or rendering the file.

Give DeckProbe the facts you need—such as the verified format, slide count, sheet names, encryption state, or asset counts—and it selects the lowest-cost valid probe path that can satisfy the requested targets and confidence.

## What DeckProbe answers

- **File identity:** verified format, MIME type, extension match, size, author, declared timestamps, and producer application.
- **Primary structure:** format-owned counts, names, dimensions, and other facts such as slides, sheets, pages, masters, or layouts where implemented.
- **Security and quality:** encryption, password protection, macros, signatures, external relationships, embedded files, active-content risk, and structural quality signals where supported.
- **Asset summaries:** images, media, previews, fonts, comments, attachments, and embedded-object counts where implemented by the selected format driver.

## What DeckProbe does not do

DeckProbe does not return a complete list of text blocks, shapes, table cells, styles, coordinates, or relationships needed to reproduce the document. Those are deep parsing concerns for [DeckOps](/products/deckops/overview).

It also does not render visual previews or mutate source files. Use [DeckRender](/products/deckrender/overview) for rendering and [DeckUse](/products/deckuse/overview) for source-aware editing.
