# DeckOps

DeckOps is the deep parsing and one-way document operations layer. Its parsing target is **Document Information**: a structured description of the file's identity, physical and logical structure, and extracted content intelligence.

After parsing, that information can be normalized into a schema-based IR for downstream analysis, transformation, or reconstruction. The detailed IR contract will be documented after the DeckOps implementation is available.

## Use DeckOps when

- A workflow needs actual text, images, tables, charts, formulas, comments, or other document objects.
- Page, slide, sheet, section, master, layout, or workbook structure must be represented rather than merely counted.
- Object positions, styles, relationships, and format-specific behavior are needed for downstream processing.
- Parsed information must be schema-normalized into an IR or reconstructed into another supported format.

## Product boundary

DeckOps is deeper than [DeckProbe](/products/deckprobe/overview): Probe returns targeted document facts and evidence, while Ops builds a content-bearing structural model.

DeckOps remains one-way in the product model. Use [DeckUse](/products/deckuse/overview) when changes must be applied through a source-aware model and written back to a supported source file. Use [DeckRender](/products/deckrender/overview) when the required result is visual output rather than structured content.

<Note>
  The Document Information model on this site describes the current product direction. Exact field names, IR schemas, supported formats, and reconstruction guarantees remain provisional until they can be verified against the DeckOps source and contracts.
